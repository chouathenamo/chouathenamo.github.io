My name is Chou Mo ("moh-choh"), and my preferred name is Athena. I am a third-year undergraduate researcher at UCLA majoring in Mathematics of Computation, with a minor in Philosophy.

I currently do research in the Hsu Lab under the supervision of Dr. William Hsu, and with Harvard Medical School under the supervision of Dr. Fangxu Xing. Previously, I was a VISE Summer Fellow at Vanderbilt University in the VINE Lab under the supervision of Dr. Daniel Moyer.

Outside of research, I co-lead the programming team for UCLA's University Rovers Challenge team. I am also a member of UCLA's Data Science Union, a competitive sailor, a skier, and more.
