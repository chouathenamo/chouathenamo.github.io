## Overview

Accurate intra-patient lung CT registration is critical for radiation therapy planning, longitudinal disease tracking, and 4D CT motion modelling. Large non-rigid deformations between expiratory (EXP) and inspiratory (INSP) breathing phases make this task  challenging, and conventional intensity-based approaches (e.g. ANTs) can lead to mismatch. This work adapts the Iterative Optimal Transport (IOT) framework (originally proposed for 2D multimodal image registration) to the 3D setting using pulmonary vessel point clouds derived from paired EXP/INSP CT scans.
